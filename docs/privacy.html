Privacy policy

Draft of 14 November 2026, not yet in force. To be completed before publishing: the contact address below.

In short

Dorie Play is a program that runs on your own computer. It has no server of its own: the developer receives no data about you, your playlists or how you use the program. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What the program keeps on your computer

Everything below is stored in the program folder on your computer and nowhere else:

  • Your API keys for the services you connect: spotify-client.json, google-client.json, soundcloud-client.json. Keys saved through the program's set-up page are readable only by your user account; a key file you put there yourself keeps the permissions you gave it.
  • Sign-ins (access tokens) given by the services after you sign in: .spotify-token, .youtube-token.json, .soundcloud-token.json. When you save a new key for a service, its old sign-in is deleted.
  • Plans in the plans folder: the name of a playlist, its songs (title, artists, length, service ids), the candidates found for each song (title, artist or channel, length, link) and your decisions. They stay until you delete them.
  • Search answers in .search-cache.json, so that the same songs are not searched twice. Answers older than 30 days are not used again and are removed the next time the program searches.
  • In your browser, the page remembers only your chosen language and look (light or dark).

What is sent, and to whom

Dorie Play connects directly from your computer to the services you choose — Spotify, YouTube (Google) and SoundCloud — with your own keys and your own sign-in, and only when you start an action:

  • reading the playlist you paste;
  • searching for each song on the service you transfer to;
  • checking whether the chosen YouTube videos still exist, when you press “Check the videos”;
  • creating a private playlist and adding the songs you approved, when you confirm the transfer.

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else. Each service handles what it receives under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy, Spotify Privacy Policy, SoundCloud Privacy Policy.

Players

When you press “Listen” next to a candidate, the page loads that service's own player (YouTube through youtube-nocookie.com, Spotify or SoundCloud). Until you press it, nothing is loaded from those services. A player is run by the service and follows its policy.

YouTube API Services

Dorie Play uses YouTube API Services. By using it with YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove Dorie Play's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page.

Removing your data

  • Delete the files and the plans folder listed above from the program folder, or delete the whole folder.
  • Remove the program's access to your accounts: for Google on the page linked above, for Spotify and SoundCloud in the connected or authorised apps section of your account settings there.
