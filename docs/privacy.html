Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026.

In short

Dorie Play is a program that runs on your own computer. It talks to the services you choose directly from your computer. Two small Dorie Play servers help: the song base and the SoundCloud sign-in server. Neither keeps logs or stores anything about you, and the developer keeps no data about you, your playlists or how you use the program. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What the program keeps on your computer

Everything below is stored in the program’s data folder on your computer and nowhere else. That is the program folder when you run Dorie Play from its source code; for the installed program it is ~/Library/Application Support/Dorie Play on the Mac and, on Windows, the app’s private storage that Windows gives every Microsoft Store app: the program asks for %APPDATA%\Dorie Play, and Windows keeps it in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\…\LocalCache\Roaming\Dorie Play. Windows deletes that storage when you uninstall the app or reset it (Settings → Apps → Dorie Play → Advanced options → Reset).

  • Your API keys for the services you connect: spotify-client.json, google-client.json. Keys saved through the program's set-up page are readable only by your user account; a key file you put there yourself keeps the permissions you gave it.
  • Sign-ins (access tokens) given by the services after you sign in: .spotify-token, .youtube-token.json, .soundcloud-token.json. When you save a new key for a service, its old sign-in is deleted.
  • Plans that involve SoundCloud lose the SoundCloud song details (titles, artists, uploader names and links) once the transfer has finished without skipped songs, or, for the plans on the start page, when you sign out of SoundCloud in the program.
  • Plans in the plans folder: the name of a playlist, its songs (title, artists, length, service ids), the candidates found for each song (title, artist or channel, length, link) and your decisions. They stay until you delete them.
  • Search answers in .search-cache.json, so that the same songs are not searched twice. Answers older than 30 days are not used again and are removed the next time the program searches.
  • In your browser, the page remembers only your chosen language and look (light or dark), whether you hid the tips, and that it has greeted you once. The program’s own window keeps the same, plus the cookies a player sets when you press “Listen”, in its own web storage: the webview folder in the data folder on Windows, the system’s web storage for the program on the Mac (~/Library/WebKit).
  • A start-up log, startup.log, only when the program cannot start: the error, without song names, keys or links. If the data folder cannot be written, the log goes to the system’s temporary folder as dorieplay-startup.log instead, and stays there until the system or you clear that folder.

What is sent, and to whom

Dorie Play connects directly from your computer to the services you choose — Spotify, YouTube (Google) and SoundCloud — and only when you start an action. Spotify and YouTube use your own keys. SoundCloud uses Dorie Play’s own SoundCloud app, so you only sign in. A public Deezer playlist you paste is read from Deezer without a key or sign-in:

  • reading the playlist you paste;
  • searching for each song on the service you transfer to;
  • checking whether the chosen YouTube videos still exist, when you press “Check the videos”;
  • unless you turn the song base off under Services on the start page: the title, artists and length of each song, sent to the song base server to find a YouTube video without a YouTube search. It keeps no logs and stores nothing about you; to limit how often one internet address may ask, it counts requests per address in memory only, and forgets the counts every hour. The videos it suggests are then checked with YouTube to see that they still play;
  • to the Dorie Play sign-in server for SoundCloud (base.dorieplay.com), when you sign in to SoundCloud and about once an hour while you use it: the one-time sign-in code, or the token that renews your sign-in. The server adds Dorie Play’s SoundCloud app key, passes the request to SoundCloud and returns SoundCloud’s answer to your computer. These tokens give access to your SoundCloud account and may contain account details. The server keeps no logs and stores nothing; to limit how often one internet address may ask, it counts requests per address in memory only, and forgets the counts every hour. Everything else goes from your computer to SoundCloud directly;
  • the program in its own window asks GitHub (api.github.com) once at start whether a newer version is out; GitHub sees your internet address. The Microsoft Store version does not ask: the Store updates it;
  • creating a playlist and adding the songs you approved, when you confirm the transfer. On YouTube and SoundCloud the playlist is private. Spotify makes playlists created by apps public: anyone with the link can open it, and it shows your Spotify profile name. You can make it private in the Spotify app.

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else. Each service handles what it receives under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy, Spotify Privacy Policy, SoundCloud Privacy Policy, Deezer Privacy Policy.

Players

When you press “Listen” next to a candidate, the page loads that service's own player (YouTube through youtube-nocookie.com, Spotify or SoundCloud). Until you press it, nothing is loaded from those services. A player is run by the service and follows its policy.

YouTube API Services

Dorie Play uses YouTube API Services. By using it with YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove Dorie Play's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page.

Removing your data

  • SoundCloud: press Sign out next to SoundCloud on the start page. It removes Dorie Play’s access to your SoundCloud account, deletes the sign-in on this computer and removes SoundCloud song details from the plans on the start page. If SoundCloud cannot be reached, the program says so; then remove Dorie Play under Connected apps in your SoundCloud settings. Plan files you saved elsewhere with the command line keep their details until you delete them.
  • Delete the files and the plans folder listed above from the data folder, or delete the whole folder. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version removes its data folder.
  • Remove the program's access to your accounts: for Google on the page linked above, for Spotify and SoundCloud in the connected or authorised apps section of your account settings there.
