Dorie Play for the Mac

For Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer). Dorie Play is not yet signed by Apple, so the first start takes two extra steps. The exact wording differs a little between macOS versions.

1. Install

Open DoriePlay-mac.dmg and drag Dorie Play into Applications.

2. First start

Open Dorie Play from Applications. The Mac says Apple could not verify it. Press Done.

3. Allow it once

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to “Dorie Play was blocked”, press Open Anyway, enter your password and press Open Anyway again.

After that Dorie Play opens like any other program. Each new version asks once again. Before you install a new version, close Dorie Play. Your keys, sign-ins and plans are kept in ~/Library/Application Support/Dorie Play and stay when you update. Read the privacy policy.
